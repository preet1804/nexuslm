Our Culinary Discipline

No shortcuts. Wood embers. Heritage botanicals.

Every dish at Saffron & Sage is an ode to patience. We source cold-pressed oils from organic Saurashtra groves, saffron directly from Pampore farmers, and stoneground grains from ancient cultivars.

Our hearth burns seasoned applewood and charcoal, producing gentle caramelization and depth that modern electric cooking can never replicate.

"Cooking with fire forces honesty. There is nothing between the heat, the seasonal produce, and the guest's palate." — Chef Aanand Vardhan, Executive Culinary Director
